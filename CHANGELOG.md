# Changelog

All notable changes to Apex Luxury Automobile Showroom platform.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-11
### Added
- Interactive 3D Studio with Three.js and Draco decompression
- TopView MCP (Mode Control Panel) with orthographic CAD blueprints and millimeter calipers
- Dynamic 3D archetypes for Luxury SUVs (G-Class), Supercars (Aventador), Sedans (Rolls-Royce), and Coupes
- 360 Turntable exterior stage with live azimuth compass HUD and 8 angle presets
- 360 Cockpit spherical panorama with 4 dedicated viewpoints and 5 supercar ambient LED moods
- Zero-overlap floating action layout for WhatsApp Desk and VIP Live Assistant
- Full Admin CMS with Lead Kanban pipeline, 3D Digital Twin configurator, and fleet management
